# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-23

## Summary
This is the best week the lab has had in a month — and I want to be precise about why, because it's not flattery. Three juries running asked for two things: a genuinely two-person embodied piece, and warmth as a standing lane instead of a one-off token. Both landed. `24360-duet` is the lab's first piece where the *coupling between two bodies* is the instrument, and it stacks four ambition criteria — only the third piece in the window to do so. And the warm pole went from one exhibit (emberfield) to three (emberfield, foreshadow, duet), each on a *different* technique, exactly as asked. The ruts that remain are real but narrower: the "sweep a cursor → re-filter one take" meta-verb still dominates (~9×), raw-WebGL2 has quietly become the new crutch substrate (4×) now that Canvas2D and three.js are banned, and — the one that matters most — the lab's single most social piece ships with its live two-body camera path **unverified**. We fixed the shape. We have not yet proven the shape works when Karel opens it.

- **Over-represented input:** single-person "one body/hand/face + pointer, waving at one looping take" grammar (**13×** of 15 — only `roundtable` and `duet` escape it). Sensors stay exempt per the 2026-09-16 embodied directive; what's flagged is the *grammar*, and this week it finally cracked twice.
- **Over-represented output:** `Canvas2D` (**6×** — mouthvalve, physforge, faultline, datum, roundtable, duet). AND the escape hatches are now crowded too: `three.js` (**4×** — mosaicfield, chamber, pitchlattice, emberfield) and `raw-WebGL2` (**4×** — vantage, priorbody, sever, foreshadow). The "break the substrate" move just migrated the monoculture into WebGL2 — only `fluxmesh` (WebGPU, 1×) is genuinely alone.
- **Over-represented technique:** "sweep a cursor → re-process one looping take" meta-verb (**~9×** — mouthvalve, physforge, faultline, datum, mosaicfield, chamber, pitchlattice, vantage, +priorbody's loop). The sub-verb rotates honestly; the meta-verb is still the lab's default reflex. BUT: 5 of 15 now break it (sever, fluxmesh, roundtable, foreshadow, duet) vs 2 last jury — real movement.
- **Over-represented vibe:** cool-clinical / schematic / datamatics (**7×** — datum, pitchlattice, mosaicfield, chamber, priorbody, sever, fluxmesh; vantage adjacent). Still the dominant register, but no longer the *only* one.
- **BANNED for next cycle:** `Canvas2D` output · a 5th `raw-WebGL2` volumetric/raymarch/GPGPU field (it is now what three.js was) · a 5th `three.js` fly-through · cool-clinical/datamatics palette · the "sweep cursor → re-filter his take" meta-verb · the single-person-waving-at-one-take grammar as the *only* interaction model. Sensors remain exempt.

## Standouts (positive)
- **24360-duet**: the week's headline and a genuine lab-first. The instrument isn't two parameters side-by-side — it's the *correlation between two people's motion*, made audible (voices converge + reverb swell) and visible (two light-fields merge to one warm-gold core). Named-ref grounded (Hove & Risen 2009), warm, social, and designed as cycle 1 of a two-camera arc. This is the direction the lab has been circling for a month, finally built.
- **23120-sever**: still the freshest *verb* — median-filter HPSS, the lab's only true source-separation. Pulling a recording into independent perceptual layers instead of re-coloring it whole. Mine this seam: new things to *do* to his music.
- **23440-fluxmesh**: still the technical summit — WebGPU-compute mass-spring physics driving a real DSP graph. The only piece escaping the WebGL2/Canvas2D/three.js gravity well. Its *method* is the template, not the membrane.
- **24120-foreshadow**: the quiet win. It took the analytical prediction verb and refused to render it as a datamatics readout — chords arrive as warm density *weather*, surprise as a felt rupture. Proof that a "cool" verb can live on the warm pole. Exactly the "make warmth a lane" ask, answered with a new technique rather than a repeat of curl-noise.
- **23680-emberfield**: still the clearest *experience* — a ten-minute room with real long-form state evolution. It started the warm lane that foreshadow and duet extended.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **21600-chamber**: auto-convolution is elegant, but it's "fly a cursor through a volume to re-filter his take" — the exact grammar + substrate now banned. Median of the window.
- **20640-mouthvalve**: the mouth sensor is a real idea stranded on a 2-scalar lowpass+tilt. The novel input deserved a richer consequence than "open mouth = brighter."
- **21280-mosaicfield / 21920-pitchlattice**: both accomplished, both the "three.js volume you fly a cursor through to re-process one take" pattern. Concept-level, they are the substrate rut the audit flags — keep the engines, retire the fly-through-to-re-filter frame.
- **20960-faultline** (as ambition, not as idea): the USGS real-world-data stance is the rarest thing here and should be mined again — but the engine underneath is a spectral-freeze re-color. Keep the *data-as-input* ambition; give it a verb that isn't re-coloring.
