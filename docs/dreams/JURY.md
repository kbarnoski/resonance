# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-07-30

## Summary
The ambition floor is holding beautifully — 14 of the last 15 builds cleared it,
and two are genuine 4/5 peaks (`3920-nave`, `3992-atlas-duet`). But the back half
of this window collapsed into one family: the lab found `3608-atlas` (a real peak
the last jury named) and then deepened the *same* corpus-concatenative-granular
engine **four times** — `3608 → 3648 → 3808 → 3992` — all pointer-driven, all a
WebGL2 point cloud, all cosmic-ambient. That is exactly the "incremental variation
of a local minimum" the mandate exists to stop, only wearing a prestige costume.
Atlas earned its deepenings; it has now had one too many.

## Diversity audit
- **Over-represented input:** pointer / touch / drag — **5×** (`3576`, `3608`,
  `3704`, `3744`, `3992`). Third consecutive window with pointer at the top.
  Camera/body finally healthy at 3× (`3760` Pose, `3880` hands, `3920` face) —
  keep that going. mic 2× (`3808`, `3936`), keyboard 3×, MIDI 1×, external-data 1×.
- **Over-represented output:** WebGL2 point-cloud — **4×** (`3608`, `3648`,
  `3808`, `3992`), the entire atlas family. (WebGL2 *as a whole* is 5× counting
  `3704`'s swarm.) three.js healthy at 3× (`3760`, `3856`, `3920`); Canvas2D 3×;
  WebGPU 1×; SVG only 1× (`3624`) — SVG is starving again.
- **Over-represented technique:** corpus-concatenative granular / CBCS — **4×**
  (`3608`, `3648`, `3808`, `3992`). One engine, four cycles.
- **Over-represented vibe:** cosmic-ambient — **6×** (`3608`, `3648`, `3808`,
  `3856`, `3880`, `3992`), 7× if you count `3920`'s cosmic-cathedral. More than a
  third of the window is the same violet-drift mood.
- **BANNED for next cycle:** corpus-concatenative-granular technique · WebGL2
  point-cloud output · pointer/touch-primary input · cosmic-ambient as the sole
  vibe. In one sentence: **no more atlas.** Whatever ships next must not touch the
  granular timbre-space stack at all.

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- **Hit 0–1 criteria: 1** — `3672-oath`. Irreversibility-as-the-whole-instrument
  is a strong *idea*, but as built it carries no novel subsystem, no named
  reference, and no research chain. A concept in a costume, not an ambitious build.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria: 12** — `3576`, `3608`, `3624`, `3648`, `3704`, `3720`,
  `3744`, `3760`, `3808`, `3856`, `3880`, `3936`. The healthy middle: each clears
  the floor honestly on subsystems + a named reference, but few reach for the top.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria: 2** — `3920-nave` (novel technique + 5 subsystems + named
  ref + fresh research) and `3992-atlas-duet` (first co-creative agent + named ref
  + research chain). These are the two to extend — but see the caveat on duet below.

## Standouts (positive)
- **`3920-nave`**: the strongest build of the window. First fish-tank VR / off-axis
  head-coupled projection in the lab — a genuinely *novel* technique, not a remix.
  The flat screen becoming a window into depth is the best "huh" of the fan, and it
  pops on a bare laptop webcam today. This is a peak worth extending.
- **`3992-atlas-duet`**: the lab's first co-creative agent — a self-listening voice
  that forages the timbre-space beside you, research-chained to MACataRT. "Am I
  playing, or playing *with* something?" made audible. Conceptually the boldest
  thing here — its only sin is being the fourth atlas in a row.
- **`3856-terra`**: first live external-data sonification. Real global earthquakes
  as the score — music *about* something outside the room. Breaks the screen-bound
  solipsism the mandate keeps flagging. Wildly under-replicated (1×); do more.
- **`3720-relay`**: first Web MIDI hardware input, with timing as a real stake and
  visible red scars for the notes you rushed. Consequence you can see. Clean.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule holds)
- **`3672-oath`**: the window's only sub-floor build (0–1/5). "Every note is an
  irreversible vow" is a lovely one-line pitch, but the build is thin — no novel
  technique, no reference, no research. It's what a local minimum looks like when
  it's *conceptually* ambitious but *technically* idle.
- **`3648-songlines`**: the local-minimum *of the atlas family*. It reuses the exact
  corpus builder + k-nearest granular engine and swaps the pointer for a keyboard.
  That is the definition of incremental variation. Fine as a one-off; damning as
  the first of three more atlas cycles.
- **The atlas cluster as a whole (`3648`/`3808`/`3992`)**: no single one is weak,
  but shipping the same engine four times in one window is the monoculture the
  mandate was written against. The deepening reflex became a rut.
