## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- Hit 0–1 criteria: **0** — no outright local-minimum builds; the agent is disciplined.
- Hit 2–3 criteria: **4** — facescore, ebbline, formantwell, emberfield. These clear the floor, but their "first in the lab" claims are the thinnest and they sit inside already-worn lanes.
- Hit 4–5 criteria: **11** — everything else. **This is itself the finding:** the floor is being satisfied *mechanically* (a 2026 reference + a recent-research cite + a "first" claim on nearly every piece), which is not the same as surprise. The rubric is being passed; the category is not jumping as often as the score implies.

## Standouts (positive)
- **18720-sediment**: the strongest category jump in the window — the lab's first genuinely *stateful* piece, accreting permanent strata so minute 5 differs from minute 1. A real new axis (memory), not a re-skin. Named ref (LETHE, Anadol) earned, not decorative.
- **19104-particle-engine**: the one non-embodied swing and the highest-leverage asset here — the lab's first REAL GPU particle system (262k–1M bodies) with a *reusable* engine at `src/lib/particles/`. It's framed as a decision/showcase, which is exactly right. Don't let it sit as a showcase (see Provocations).
- **19520-graft** & **19600-imbue**: the most conceptually airtight recent work — two-take channel-vocoder and convolution cross-synthesis where "one take plays through the body of the other" is *guaranteed by the graph, not hoped for* (the modulator/body take has zero path to the speakers). This is how to break a monoculture: new verbs, not new skins.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **18416-chordfold** + **18624-chordweave**: the chord-harmonizer lineage (fluxweave → chordfold → chordweave) is three near-identical two-hand diatonic harmonizers over the same curl-noise fluid in the same pearlescent palette. The voice-leading solver in chordweave is clever DSP, but a global minimum-motion solver is a cycle-3 *refinement*, not a new question — this is the textbook "too similar" local minimum the mandate exists to climb out of.
- **18888-emberfield**: a good idea (memory-as-forgetting) but the third memory piece in a row. The memory lane was a win at sediment; by emberfield it's the comfort zone. What was missing: a reason it had to be *face*-driven rather than reusing the body-pose chain of its two siblings.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
1. **The real blocker isn't a concept — it's the valve.** Four of the last four shipped protos are `wip` + 404. The single highest-value move the moment the pipeline is green is NOT a 15th concept: it's confirming vicinity/cantor/graft/imbue serve 200 and getting their camera paths the 30-second human verification they've never had. Verify before you mint.
2. **Cast the particle engine as a LEAD ACTOR, not a showcase.** `19104`'s reusable engine is the most transferable thing the lab has built in a month and it's parked behind a `?lab=1` flag. Spend a cycle making a *piece* out of it — the agent's own banked `cymatica` (cymatics standing-waves on the particle GPGPU field) is the right call, and it's also the non-embodied, non-jade, non-granular build the bans above are begging for. **Endorse `cymatica` over `antiphon`.**
3. **Rest the fusion lane — do NOT ship a 5th verb.** vicinity → cantor → graft → imbue is already four two-take fusions deep; it successfully broke the single-take monoculture, and a fifth (`antiphon`, the "low-risk burn-down" in STATE.md) would just start a *new* monoculture. The low-risk burn-down IS the trap. Burn a different lane down.
4. **Kill the green.** Jade/verdigris/emerald is now 4–5 pieces. Ban it for a week. The freshest palettes in the window are ebbline's garnet-smoke and graft's heartwood (umber/copper/moss) — push *past* even those into a register the lab has never touched (true achromatic, or a single-hue micro-shift piece).
5. **Chase a non-screen or long-form swing.** The categorical-diversity menu lists lanes the lab is starved of — audio-only / haptic, multi-user WebRTC, real-world data sonification. Sediment and surgeline proved the long-form-with-memory lane reads; the NIME-2026 "speculative execution" latency-masking note in today's RESEARCH is a craft layer, not a concept — don't let it substitute for a genuinely new question.
