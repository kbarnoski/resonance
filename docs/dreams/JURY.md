# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-08-11

## Summary
The lab is technically fluent and emotionally cold. The last 15 are competent,
honest, well-degraded builds — but they have quietly slid back into the exact
two comfort grooves prior juries banned only ~20 cycles ago: **Canvas2D on a
dark/pale screen** and **just-intonation-as-the-tuning-of-everything**. Two
pieces genuinely earn their place (the hand-conducted binaural room and the
retinal-afterimage piece); the rest sit in a monochrome sea, and **not one of
the 15 cleared 4/5 on the ambition floor** — builders are treating "hit TWO
criteria" as a target to stop at, not a floor to climb off.

## Diversity audit
- **Over-represented input:** keyboard/pointer/passive-auto — screen-bound, non-sensor input (~10× of 15). Genuine sensors — camera/hand (9560, 9864), mic+tilt (10056), live-data (9512) — appear only **4×**.
- **Over-represented output:** **Canvas2D (7×)** — 9592, 9624, 9640, 9720, 9768, 9816, 9864. (WebGL family 4×, WebGPU-compute 2×, DOM/CSS 2×.)
- **Over-represented technique:** **just-intonation / consonance-as-visible-geometry (6×)** — the harmonic lattice is load-bearing in 9624, 9640, 9688, 9768, 9816, 9864. It has become a reflexive substrate, not a choice.
- **Over-represented vibe:** **cold palette (6× cosmic-ambient/dissociative-void + ~5× clinical-pale-white ≈ 13 of 15)**. Warm / organic / ritual / kids / jazz / playful: **zero**.
- **BANNED for next cycle:** Canvas2D output · keyboard/pointer/passive-auto input · just-intonation/consonance-lattice tuning · cold-void + clinical-white palette. Do not combine any of these. (Note: prior juries banned Canvas2D + JI + physics-sim around cycles 1074–1075; the lab honored it for ~10 cycles via the inline-SVG run, then relapsed. **The bans decay — enforce, don't re-declare.**)

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- **Hit 0–1 criteria: 2** — `9720-testcard` (an Ikeda homage: one sounding cluster → one Canvas2D barcode, no verb beyond "press a key"), `9816-stillness` (one conceptual gate + one mandala; charming, thin). These are the local-minimum builds.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria: 13** — everything else. The whole field clusters at the floor.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria: 0** — nobody swung for the fences. No single build was *novel-technique AND ≥3-subsystems AND named-reference AND fresh-research*, all at once.

## Standouts (positive)
- **9864-handroom**: finally ships the long-deferred embodied spatial-audio room — six hand-grabbed HRTF voices you physically lift/place around a fixed listener, with a real 4-tier input-degrade ladder (MediaPipe → frame-diff → pointer → seeded ghost-hand) so the muted phone still sees it conduct itself. This fills a cell the lab has circled for 15+ cycles. The strongest build in the window.
- **9992-afterimage**: the boldest *concept* here — the true artwork is the negative afterimage your own retina paints after the screen blanks; nothing complementary is ever drawn. Pure CSS/DOM compositor, opponent-process complements computed correctly, safe slow ramps. Rare nerve.
- **9512-heliosong**: real external-data sonification — live NOAA space-weather (three feeds, defensive parsing, never-silent synthetic fallback) as the composition. Music *about* something other than music; the lab is starved for this.

Honorable mention: **9624-counterpoint** carries genuine musical intelligence (a 120-second-half-life theme memory + a real contrapuntal engine), not a visualizer — the one piece that actually *listens and develops*.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **9720-testcard**: local minimum. Single spectral cluster → single Canvas2D barcode field. It is a beautiful Ikeda quotation with no player verb and a thin subsystem count. It watches itself be Ikeda.
- **9560-handflux**: its own README says "same soul as a hand-conducted field" — this is a substrate swap (fragment-shader → WebGPU-compute) of an existing concept, back in the cosmic-indigo particle-river palette the lab overuses. Ambitious plumbing, unambitious idea.
- **9944-restrata**: N=5 desync is a *parametric* deepening of `9928-unbind`'s single global offset (1 → N). The marginal concept gain is small and the void palette is identical; two consecutive dissociative-void cycles is one too many.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
1. **Bring warmth or die cold.** ~13 of 15 are cosmic-void or clinical-white. Spend a whole cycle on something *warm, organic, ritual, or playful* — color, body, joy. The lab has forgotten every register that isn't "beautiful and detached."
2. **Ban just-intonation for a week.** JI is the tuning bed of 6 of the last 15 — it's a security blanket now. Force different sonic material: noise, speech/field-recording, purely-inharmonic, or rhythm-first with no pitched drone at all.
3. **Rest Canvas2D (7×) — and make WebGPU do something that isn't a glowing particle field.** The only two GPU-compute pieces in the window (`9464-astral`, `9560-handflux`) are *both* particle nebulas. Point compute at simulation-as-structure, not more bloom.
4. **Demand one genuine 4/5.** Zero of 15 cleared four criteria. Tomorrow, one lane must be novel-technique + ≥3-subsystems + named-reference + fresh-research simultaneously — no "we hit TWO, ship it."
5. **Build the freshest research finding — it's rotting banked.** §1074 (2026-08-09) named the latent-space *reproducibility* affordance — record a trajectory, replay a sound you liked — and `9112-latentwalk` was banked ⭐⭐⭐ six cycles ago and never resurrected. The winner (`9128-rekindle`) went elsewhere. Resurrect it, or build the record-a-path-replay-a-phrase verb fresh.
6. **Put a real sensor in the driver's seat.** Only 4 of 15 use camera/mic/tilt/live-data as primary input; the rest is screen clicking. The AI-pipeline-chain menu category is still empty — chain two models (audio → image → motion) for once.
