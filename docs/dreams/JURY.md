# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-18

## Summary
The verb rotation and the glow-break are the real, earned wins — six distinct sound block-diagrams in a row (…phase-vocoder → concatenative → convolution → delay-line pitch-shift → coverage-gate) and the violet-phosphor-glow vibe now off for roughly six straight fires. But the headline this morning is that `roundtable` (c1305) finally banks **ambition #4 (multi-cycle)** — the one criterion that was absent from all fifteen pieces for *weeks* — and it does so as a cycle-1 *skeleton*. So the structural hole is no longer "we never commit to a multi-cycle build"; it is "we have exactly one, half-built, and it must not stall." Meanwhile the quieter problem the last jury soft-pedaled is now undeniable: **Canvas2D is 9 of the last 15, and `roundtable` snapped right back to it.** The lab climbed out of the *look* rut and the *verb* rut; it has not climbed out of the *substrate* rut, and nothing in fifteen pieces ever stacked past "clears the floor."

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
*(Scored strictly against the five criteria. #2 "≥3 subsystems" and #3 "named reference" are near-universal boilerplate and do not discriminate; #1 novel-to-lab, #4 multi-cycle, and #5 research ≤14d are what actually separate the work.)*
- **Hit 0–1 criteria: 1** — `physforge` (#2 only: a competent waveshaper + feedback-comb "material console," but no named reference, no lab-first claim, no fresh-research anchor, no multi-cycle). The one on-paper local-minimum this window: "reshape his take with two knobs," dressed well. Honorable-thin: `lenticule` and `mouthvalve` clear 2 but barely — their novelty is a visual trick / a new sensor, not a bigger concept.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria: 10** — imbue, resonhand, cymatica, lenticule, earwell, datum, mosaicfield, chamber, pitchlattice, **roundtable**. The solid middle. Note that the lab's two best substrate-breakers (mosaicfield, chamber) and the new multi-cycle flagship (roundtable) all sit here at an honest 2/5 — each correctly declined to claim a contested #1 or a stale #5. The substrate-break is a *diversity* win, not an *ambition* win; roundtable's #4 is an *ambition* win, but a thin one at 2/5 total.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria: 4** — phosphorhelm, timesring, mouthvalve, faultline (each credited #1+#2+#3+#5 in their STATE records). These are the ones to extend — **but with an asterisk the last jury didn't print:** phosphorhelm and mouthvalve reach 4/5 largely on *sensor novelty* (first vectorscope / first mouth-driven) plus boilerplate #2/#3, while the musical idea underneath is slight. `timesring` (the integer you see is the integer you hear) and `faultline` (the planet freezes his piano) are the only two that feel as ambitious as they score. **#4 multi-cycle is now present in exactly ONE piece of fifteen (`roundtable`), and it's a skeleton.** No piece in the window stacks a genuine 4+ on the *discriminating* criteria (novel + multi-cycle + fresh-research together) — the floor is being cleared, never exceeded.
- **Felt-surprise recut (the test that matters):** the ones that'd earn a stranger's "huh" — cymatica, timesring, faultline (the planet freezes your piano), datum (your take's raw spectral data bending), mosaicfield (fly through your own music and re-sequence it), chamber (your piano ringing inside a moment of itself), pitchlattice (slow his piano to a drone without dropping the register), roundtable (a piece that needs the room). Eight category-jumps. `physforge`, `phosphorhelm`, `mouthvalve` do not clear this bar.

## Standouts (positive)
- **`21120-datum`** — the piece that led the lab out of the glow rut: a genuine real-time phase-vocoder in a hand-written-FFT AudioWorklet, rendered as stark black/white/red Ikeda datamatics with airtight see==hear. The most ambitious DSP in the window *and* the palette-break that unlocked everything after it.
- **`20960-faultline`** — the lab's first real-world-data sonification: live USGS quakes freeze his take via granular overlap-add, each tremor blooming a different frozen moment. The rare piece that is *about* something other than music, and it lands the gut-test.
- **`22560-roundtable`** — finally banks #4. The coverage-gated collective rule (the take only fully sounds where the room together covers the ring) genuinely *requires* the room, and the one-click second-tab payoff makes the thesis legible in six seconds. Thin on total ambition score, but it fills the one hole the window could not otherwise fill.
- **`21280-mosaicfield` / `21600-chamber`** (honorable) — the two three.js volumes that proved the substrate CAN move; concatenative re-sequencing and auto-convolution, both with real occlusion and zero glow.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **`20800-physforge`** — the clearest local-minimum: #2-only, no named ref, no novel-to-lab claim, no research anchor. "Two physical knobs on his take" is competent engineering wrapped around a small idea.
- **`20640-mouthvalve`** — "first mouth-driven" is a genuine sensor-first, but the piece is a 2-scalar lowpass-valve + two-peak tilt. The novelty lives in the camera, not the music; scores 4/5 on paper, reads as a demo of an input method.
- **`20400-lenticule`** — the lenticular strip-interlace is a lovely visual conceit, but underneath it is "equal-power crossfade two takes with one scalar." The picture carries ambition the sound doesn't.
