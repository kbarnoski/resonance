# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-22

## Summary
The lab listened. Yesterday's verdict asked for a warm, cosmic-ambient, screen-light break before "stack 4" hardened into a rut, and this morning's fire delivered exactly that — `23680-emberfield`, a ten-minute ember-room you stand inside, is the first genuinely warm, meditative, boundless piece in the whole window and it's a good one. Credit where due: that's a fast, honest turn. But one swallow isn't summer. Emberfield is **one** warm piece standing against **seven** cool-clinical ones; it's still **one person at a laptop waving at one looping take**; and the moment you widen the lens past the palette, the deeper ruts the last three juries named are all still here — zero audio-only pieces, zero two-person pieces, three.js has quietly become the new Canvas2D, and nearly every embodied piece in the window ships *demoable* with its core camera interaction marked "untested — needs your 30-second check." We fixed the color. We haven't fixed the shape.

## Diversity audit
- **Over-represented input:** single-person control — "one body/hand/face + pointer, waving at one looping take" (**14×** of 15; only `roundtable` is multi-user). Per the 2026-09-16 embodied directive the camera/body/face *sensors* are exempt — what's flagged is the *grammar*: one person, one take, re-shape-by-gesture.
- **Over-represented output:** `Canvas2D` (**6×** — lenticule, mouthvalve, physforge, faultline, datum, roundtable) — AND `three.js` has crept to the same line (**4×** — mosaicfield, chamber, pitchlattice, emberfield). three.js is becoming the next default substrate right as Canvas2D cools off.
- **Over-represented technique:** "sweep a cursor → re-process one looping take" — spectral/filter/convolution/granular re-coloring of a single recording (**≥12×**; essentially everything except roundtable's networked coverage). The sub-verb rotates diligently (bandpass → waveshaper → convolution → phase-vocoder → HPSS → physics-DSP), but the *meta*-verb hasn't moved in the whole window.
- **Over-represented vibe:** cool-clinical / schematic / Ikeda-datamatics (**7×** — datum, pitchlattice, mosaicfield, chamber, priorbody, sever, fluxmesh). The intense/analytical pole still dominates; the cosmic-ambient pole is `emberfield` alone.
- **BANNED for next cycle:** `Canvas2D` output · a 5th `three.js` volumetric fly-through · cool-clinical/schematic/datamatics palette · the single-person "wave at one looping take" grammar as the *only* interaction model · "sweep cursor → re-filter his take" as the core verb. Sensors stay exempt; what's banned is doing these together one more time.

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- **Hit 0–1 criteria: 4** — lenticule, earwell, mouthvalve, chamber. Local-minimum builds: a single scalar swept over one take through one transform. Often charming and self-explanatory, but incremental.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria: 9** — physforge, faultline, datum, mosaicfield, pitchlattice, roundtable (3), priorbody (3), vantage (3), emberfield (3). The honest middle; the recent ones rest on #5+#2+#3 with #1/#4 declined truthfully.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria: 2** — sever (#1 lab-first HPSS + #2 + #3 + #5) and fluxmesh (#1 lab-first GPU-compute→audio-param + #2 + #4 multi-cycle + #5). The two pieces that stacked the discriminating criteria. Extend the *method*, not the specific pieces.

## Standouts (positive)
- **23440-fluxmesh**: still the summit — a WebGPU-compute mass-spring membrane whose emergent physics drives a real Web-Audio DSP graph. A genuinely new substrate. But it's shipped; extending it further now is the "fancier local minimum" the agent correctly refused this morning.
- **23120-sever**: the freshest *verb* in the window. Median-filter HPSS is the lab's first real source-separation — pulling a recording into independent perceptual layers instead of re-coloring it whole — and the Gray-Scott reaction-diffusion field is a new visual substrate too. This is the direction to mine: new things to *do* to his music, not new ways to filter it.
- **22560-roundtable**: the only non-single-person piece in the entire window, and the first build in lab history to reach cycle 3. Cooperative, social, warm. It proves the multi-person idea works — but it's pointer-based. It is *begging* for an embodied, two-camera sequel.
- **23680-emberfield**: the warm turn, shipped the morning after it was asked for. A room you inhabit for ten minutes, with real long-form state evolution (the flow topology re-grows over minutes). The one piece that is an experience, not an analysis.
- **20480-earwell**: quietly the closest thing to an audio-only / sonification-first piece — "navigate into a recording by ear." Its engine is the lab's oldest verb (a bandpass bank), so it isn't ambitious, but its *stance* (listening as the interface, the screen as afterthought) is the rarest and most under-explored thing here.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **20400-lenticule**: a lovely optical trick, but underneath it's two takes and one crossfade scalar on Canvas2D — a single-scalar local minimum dressed up as a print.
- **20640-mouthvalve**: the mouth sensor is a genuinely good idea stranded on a thin engine — a 2-parameter lowpass+tilt. The novel input deserved a richer consequence than "open mouth = brighter."
- **21600-chamber**: auto-convolution is elegant, but it's "fly a cursor through a three.js volume to re-filter his take" — exactly the grammar + substrate now double-banned. The concept is sound; the execution is the median of the window.
- **20480-earwell** (as a *build*, not a stance): the bandpass-bank engine is the oldest, most-worn verb in the lab. Keep the audio-first *ambition*; retire the filterbank *mechanism*.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
- **Build something with no screen.** The deepest rut isn't a palette or a substrate — it's that every piece is a *picture*. The lab has zero true audio-only / eyes-closed / headphone-only pieces (earwell is the closest and it still has a screen). Spend one cycle testing the screen bias directly: an embodied piece you experience with your eyes shut, where the body conducts and the only output is spatial sound.
- **Two bodies, one take.** `roundtable` proved cooperative works; the embodied directive wants camera motion; nothing in fifteen pieces combines them. Build the two-person embodied piece — two people (two cameras, or two in one frame) conducting one of Karel's takes *together*, where togetherness is the reward. This has been the unbuilt #4-multi-cycle+social idea for three juries running.
- **Make warmth a lane, not a token.** `emberfield` is one warm piece against seven cool ones. Do NOT let it be a one-off — but do NOT repeat curl-noise either. The next cosmic-ambient piece should use a *different* warm technique (a near-death tunnel-to-light, a meditative void that empties rather than fills, slow luminance drift) so the warm/ambient pole becomes a standing alternative, not a single exhibit.
- **three.js is the new Canvas2D — treat it as banned too.** Four three.js volumetric fly-throughs in the window (mosaicfield, chamber, pitchlattice, emberfield). The next GPU piece should extend fluxmesh's *method* (WebGPU compute that produces or drives audio) or go raw-WebGL2 — not a fifth "translucent cells in FogExp2 you fly a camera through."
- **New verb, not new filter.** `sever` (separation) and `fluxmesh` (physics→param) are the only two genuinely new things *done to* his music in fifteen pieces. The banked `cadenceoracle` (predict/anticipate his next chord) is a real third verb — ship it, or pick another: sonify something *about* the music (its form, its surprise, its silence), don't sweep another cursor across another spectrogram.
