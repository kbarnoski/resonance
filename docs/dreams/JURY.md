# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-07

## Summary
The source-break held — and that is the real headline. Three fires running, the lab broke the single-take monoculture by a *different* mechanism each time: vicinity (four takes in an HRTF room), cantor (your voice as a control signal), and today graft (two takes fused into one voice by a channel vocoder). That is no longer a stunt; it is a direction, and it is the most sustained responsiveness this process has shown. But the render discipline went the *wrong* way: three.js was banned at 6× last verdict and the lab shipped cantor on three.js anyway, pushing it to **7× — the single worst over-fit in the window**. Meanwhile the palette quietly collapsed into two camps (warm-earth 5×, cool-clinical 4×), and the biggest board move — a two-person WebRTC room — is untouched for the fifth verdict running. Audio ambition is climbing; visual and structural discipline is slipping to compensate.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **cantor's and the body-conductors' three.js reflex**: cantor is a genuinely new *input* (first voice-as-control, QA-verified headless) wrapped in the single most-banned *output*. The idea is fresh; the dress is the rut. It is the clearest example this window of a good concept defaulting to three.js when a rested path (its own jade column reads fine in WebGL2 or Canvas2D) would have cleared the render ban for free.

- **The formant & harmonization trilogies** (now 2× each in-window): both are DONE. Technically excellent voice-leading and cepstral work; neither *concept* ever grew past "reshape one take with two hands." Do not start a fourth of either.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
1. **three.js is banned for the SECOND time (7×) — this time enforce it, and the enforcement is obvious: use the particle-engine.** The lab built a reusable raw-WebGL2 GPGPU particle system (`src/lib/particles/`, 262k–1M bodies, FFT-per-band) in a Karel session and has never once cast it in an *agent* cycle. Make tomorrow the first *embodied* use of it — a real take, a body signal driving the force field. That retires the three.js habit AND answers the standing "put the engine to work" note in one move.
2. **antiphon is the LAST banked source-break — burn it down, OR go bigger with the WebRTC room.** After cantor + graft shipped, `antiphon` (body conducts a call-and-response between two takes, QA-PASS, IDEAS §1281) is the only source-break left on the shelf. Shipping it draws the backlog to zero and makes four straight non-single-take cycles. The more ambitious alternative is the move that has been #1-untouched for five verdicts: **a two-person shared HRTF room over WebRTC** — multi-user (never once attempted) + spatial + a direct extension of vicinity's substrate. That is the real "massively bigger."
3. **Both palette camps are over-fit — warm-earth (5×) AND cool-clinical (4×). Ban both.** The lab didn't diversify palette; it migrated from one monoculture to a second. Force a register that is neither ochre/patina/ember NOR cyan/teal/violet: monochrome/achromatic, a single saturated non-violet hue held pure, high-key white-on-light, or vicinity's indigo→rose expanded. Make the first screen look unlike the last ten.
4. **Rest the grain hammer (4×).** sediment/rerise/gaitpulse/surgeline all re-articulate with Hann grains. graft already proved the lab can do a NON-granular transform (vocoder). Keep going off-grain: a convolution variant of graft (one take as the reverb impulse response for another — non-granular, two-source, breaks the ban again), physical-modeling resynthesis, or spectral morphing you haven't tried.
5. **The DSP trilogies are complete and aging out — do not start a third lineage of near-identical rooms.** The temptation after vicinity is a "spatial trilogy" of HRTF re-skins. Resist it exactly as you should resist a fourth formant piece. Spatial's next move is multi-user (#2), not another room.
