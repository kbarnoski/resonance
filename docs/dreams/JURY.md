# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-05

## Summary
The lab is productive, disciplined, and internally rigorous — every one of the last 15 cycles cleared the ambition floor (zero local-minimum builds at the 0–1 level), finished two cycle-3 merges instead of over-minting, and sustained a genuinely new MEMORY category across three distinct mechanisms. But the diversity audit is being won at the margins while the core is a monoculture: **15 of 15 prototypes are "one seated body, one webcam, one of Karel's pre-recorded solo piano takes, reshaped on a screen."** The team rotates the render path, the DSP verb, and the body part and calls it diversity; the deep structure — single person, single source, single screen — has not moved in two weeks. The memory lane is the real gold. Everything else is the lab polishing its comfort zone very well.

## Diversity audit
- **Over-represented input**: two-hand gesture (7×) — murmuration, timbrefold, fluxweave, throatmorph, chordfold, chordweave, formantwell. (All 15 are camera/MediaPipe — exempt per the 2026-09-16 embodied directive — but *two-hand* specifically is the over-fit axis, as prior juries flagged.)
- **Over-represented output**: WebGPU (4×: murmuration, fluxweave, chordfold, chordweave) **and** Canvas2D (4×: timbrefold, throatmorph, formantwell, emberfield). Three.js at 3×, SVG at 2×.
- **Over-represented technique**: no single DSP verb hits ≥4, but three technique-FAMILIES sit tied at 3 each — harmonization/chord-stack (fluxweave→chordfold→chordweave), formant/vocal-tract morph (timbrefold→throatmorph→formantwell), memory/stateful (sediment→rerise→emberfield). The genuine ≥4 finding is the meta-technique: **single pre-recorded piano take re-synthesised live, 15/15.** The audio SOURCE axis has been frozen for the entire window.
- **Over-represented vibe**: the cool cyan/teal/violet luminous-clinical register (≥7×: tidewell, timbrefold, throatmorph, fluxweave, chordfold, chordweave, formantwell). Warm/earth palettes (patina, ember, jade) are the minority.
- **BANNED for next cycle**: two-hand input · WebGPU output · Canvas2D output · cool cyan/teal/violet luminous-clinical palette · **single pre-recorded-piano-take as sole audio source** (the real ban this week).

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- **Hit 0–1 criteria**: 0 — genuinely none. The floor is being cleared every cycle; this is a real credit to the process.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria**: 9 — tidewell, slowbloom, timbrefold, fluxweave, throatmorph, chordfold, facescore, gaitpulse, ebbline. Solid, research-anchored, but these are the comfort-zone builds: a body part mapped to a DSP knob on one piano take, rendered prettily.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria**: 6 — murmuration (Vicsek named ref + ≥3 subsystems), chordweave & formantwell (cycle-3 merges, D), sediment, rerise, emberfield (the memory lane: novel technique + named ref + multi-cycle). **These are the ones to extend.**

## Standouts (positive)
- **18720-sediment**: the single most ambitious concept in the window — the lab's first stateful, >5-minute, genuinely-evolving piece. Different at minute 5 than minute 1 because everything you did is still physically in the column. This is what "massively bigger" looks like.
- **18816-rerise** + **18888-emberfield**: a three-cycle arc where each cycle adds a *new verb* (accretion → retrieval-reconsolidation → forgetting-by-neglect), not a re-skin. Rare restraint — most "lineages" in this lab are polish passes; this one grew the concept each time.
- **18624-chordweave** + **18656-formantwell**: the lab's first two cycle-3 *merges* — folding a banked sibling into a shipped one and drawing the backlog DOWN. Finishing discipline deserves celebrating in a shop that loves to mint.
- **18304-murmuration**: the cleanest named-reference build — it borrows the actual Vicsek order-parameter model and makes the physics itself audible (φ conducts the take's clarity). Reference used, not name-dropped.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **The formant trilogy** (timbrefold → throatmorph → formantwell): three cycles reshaping the vocal tract of a piano take with two hands. Each is a real DSP improvement, but the *concept* never grew — same input, same cool-clinical vibe, same single source. By formantwell it was a merge, not a new idea. Diminishing returns dressed as a lineage.
- **The harmonization trilogy** (fluxweave → chordfold → chordweave): same critique — three cycles fanning one take into a chord of itself, two-hand + cool-luminous throughout. Technically excellent voice-leading by the end; conceptually a local minimum.
- **facescore / ebbline**: competent SVG single-take conductors — but these are this lab's equivalent of "pentatonic-melody-plus-canvas-visualizer." A body part maps to a DSP knob, rendered cleanly. They clear the floor; they don't exceed it.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
1. **Un-freeze the SOURCE axis.** 15/15 pieces reshape ONE pre-recorded solo piano take — that is the real monoculture, not the render path. You already proved you can leave it (tremor/ISS/Wikipedia/ledger, cycles 1251–1256) and then abandoned it the moment mudra got loved. Spend a cycle where the audio source is NOT a single piano file: a live external feed, two takes in dialogue, or mic-as-secondary-over-catalog.
2. **Get off the webcam-and-screen.** The categorical menu's spatial/installation, embedded/non-screen (audio-only/haptic), and multi-user/WebRTC rows are ALL untouched this window. Zero embodied-SPATIAL prototypes exist. A depth-camera spatial-audio room, or an audio-ONLY piece, is "massively bigger" than a 16th single-seat conductor.
3. **Two-hand is banned (7×); cool cyan/teal/violet is banned (≥7×).** The memory lane already proved warm/earth palettes and full-body/face inputs ship fine — carry that out of the memory lane into a fresh mint.
4. **WebGPU and Canvas2D are each at 4× — ban both for a week.** If you need a render path, the rested shelves are projection/installation and audio-forward-only (you have had none since stillpoint, pre-window).
5. **Extend the standouts; do NOT start a fourth DSP trilogy.** The memory lane carries the ambition. A cycle-4 that makes memory SHARED between two people (WebRTC) would hit multi-user AND extend the lineage AND break the single-seat monoculture in one move — the single best "bigger concept" move on the board.
