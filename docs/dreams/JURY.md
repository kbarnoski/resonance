# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-07-31

## Summary
The ambition floor is genuinely healthy — every one of the last 15 cleared it (no
`3672-oath`-style local-minimum-in-a-costume this window), three builds truly sing
(`4120-brine`, `4056-overtone-loom`, `4264-lucent`), and the input diet finally
broke open (tilt, external-data, keyboard all got real cash). But the *theme* has
quietly re-collapsed into a new monoculture: **the lab has become a
neuroscience-of-perception gallery**. Form constants (`4032`), spatial presence
(`4088`), synesthesia (`4184`), dissociation (`4200`), migraine aura (`4216`) —
five of the last eleven are "cite a perception paper, render it as a violet AV
instrument with a references list." That is exactly the "local minimum wearing a
prestige costume" the last jury named about atlas, only now it wears a lab coat
instead of a granular cloud. The floor is not the problem anymore. The imagination
is.

  **tied with** WebGL2-shader-family — **4×** (`3992` point-cloud, `4032` shader,

  (`3920`, `4088`, `4200`), WebGPU 2× (`3880`, `4120`), SVG finally 2× (`4056`,

- **Over-represented technique:** live spectral-feature extraction (FFT
  centroid / flux / RMS as the per-frame driver) — **6×** (`3936`, `4032`, `4056`,
  `4120`, `4184`, `4264`). The surface techniques all differ (SPH, advection,

- **Over-represented vibe:** cosmic-ambient / dreamlike — **5×** (`3920`, `3992`,

- **BANNED for next cycle:** camera + audio-primary input · Canvas2D +
  WebGL2-shader-family output · live-spectral-feature-driver technique ·
  cosmic-ambient vibe · **and the "altered-states / perceptual-phenomenon-with-a-
  citations-list" concept frame** (the `_shared/psych/logpolar` engine specifically

  brains.** The next build must not explain a perceptual phenomenon.

## Standouts (positive)
- **`4120-brine`**: the strongest build of the window. First GPU SPH fluid in the
  lab — 8,192 particles, three WGSL compute passes per substep, a real physics
  solver, and it *sings back* via an atomic kinetic-energy reduction read one frame
  late. "Sing a body of water into motion" is the best huh here, and WebGPU output
  is the directest break from the shader/Canvas monoculture. Extend this.
- **`4056-overtone-loom`**: the most conceptually surprising. "A sound that
  composes its own scale" is a genuine third stance on tuning — neither imposed
  (12-TET) nor absent (pure continuous pitch): the tuning is *read out of the
  signal*. Lands the starved SVG lane, no GPU risk. Concept-forward the way the
  mandate wants.
- **`4264-lucent`**: the honest answer to the lab's thinnest cell — long-form with
  real memory. The semi-Lagrangian advection-feedback accumulator means minute 5 is
  materially not minute 1, and it finally puts Karel's *real* Path piano in the
  carrier, not a test tone. This is what "stateful, evolving" should mean.
- (Diversity nod, not a peak: **`4232-pendulum`** finally cashed tilt-input +
  SVG-output at once — the two most-starved lanes — even though its ambition is a
  self-admitted 2/5.)

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **`4216-fortress`**: beautiful rendering, but concept-level it is the *third* pass
  through `_shared/psych/logpolar` after `4032-formflight` — a migraine aura is
  Klüver's form constants seen through the same retinotopic warp. No fresh research
  chain (CSD is decades-old), no novel subsystem. This is atlas-style engine
  deepening in a new palette.
- **`4232-pendulum`**: earned its slot on phone-nativeness and starved-lane
  diversity, not on ambition — a warm harmonograph technique it honestly grades 2/5.
  Fine as a diversity move; a poor template. Do not read its 4×-"ship-next" streak
  as a signal that the *concept* was strong; it was strong *for the phone review*.
- **`4184-scrying`**: image-as-spectrogram → inverse-STFT is a well-trodden idea
  (the arXiv "Images that Sound" ref is real but the sonification path is standard
  additive read-out). No novel subsystem, no recent-research chain — a clean build
  of a known trick, which is the definition of the middle.
