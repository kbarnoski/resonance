Criteria: (1) novel technique · (2) ≥3 subsystems · (3) named reference · (4) multi-cycle · (5) research <14d.
- **Hit 0–1 criteria — the local-minimum builds:** **0.** Fourth window running with none. The floor mandate is permanently internalised; it is no longer the lever.

- **Hit 4–5 criteria — the ones to extend:** 10 — `2848-overturning` (**5/5**), `2864-stillpoint`, `2888-mesolife`, `2912-ensemble`, `2920-follow` (**5/5**), `2928-freeharmony`, `2952-tabla`, `2960-murmuration`, `2992-around`, `3080-mycelium`.

Distribution is excellent and stable (0 / 5 / 10). **The floor is not the lever — *kind* is, for the third jury straight.** Ten strong builds where half share a shader and a vibe is still a monoculture. The sharp critic this window: **"a hand on the instrument" is necessary but not sufficient. Breathing at a Ganzfeld or humming at a flower is a hand on a *dial*, not a hand on an *instrument*.** The real human-instrument peaks this window — `2920-follow`, `2952-tabla`, `2960-murmuration` — are ones where the human is *responsible for a musical decision that can be wrong*. The altered-states pieces mostly ask the human to *modulate an ambience*. Both are "input," but only one has stakes.

## Standouts (positive)
- `2920-follow` — **the piece the last jury begged to ship, shipped, and it's the real thing.** A machine that *listens to you sing live* — rubato, breaths, rushed phrases — and holds the accompaniment locked to *your* position via online forward-path DTW, waiting when you hold and catching up when you leap. This is the score-follower as a genuine reactive partner: the human owns the melody moment-to-moment, real stakes, a machine that can genuinely fall behind. The single most product-relevant piece in the lab. **Extend THIS toward a real song and a real duet.**
- `2952-tabla` — **a genuine new DSP substrate, not a new skin.** The lab's first **2-D digital waveguide mesh** (Van Duyne & Smith 1993) — every tap radiates real waves that reflect off the rim and interfere into the drum's modes, nothing sampled — plus the *ga* press-to-bend stroke that raises local membrane tension under your palm. You strike a real vibrating body with your hand. This is embodiment with a physics engine under it, the most literal reading of "hand on the instrument."
- `2960-murmuration` — **the seam nobody asked for and the freshest idea in the window.** A hand-played continuous-pitch instrument whose *memory is a stigmergic swarm* — 48 boid-voices that grow around the phrases you keep returning to and forget the ones you abandon, closing an ant-trail reinforcement loop. It is different at minute 8 than second 0 *because of what you played* — long-form memory (JURY-2026-07-26 #5) realised as emergent behaviour, not a stored buffer.
- `2992-around` — **the one piece whose output is NOT a screen.** A binaural sound-sculpture room: place sustained voices at real 3-D positions and hear them orbit your head through per-voice HRTF panners as you turn. First true spatial-audio room in the lab; the canvas is a radar, not the piece — the piece is the space. This is exactly the non-shader register the next cycle should chase.
- `2848-overturning` (5/5, carried) remains the peak of the now-retired science-sonification lane — kept as the reference for "structure you *hear coming*, not a readout you decode."

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- `3048-chrysanthemum` + `3056-clearlight` — **the local minimum of the *new* rut.** Both are "the shared `logpolar` form-constant engine, driven by one input scalar (voice / breath), blooms when you sound and collapses when you stop." The named references (Klüver, Bressloff–Cowan, Huxley, Ganzfeld) are real and the craft is clean — but neither introduces a technique the lab didn't already own from `2864-stillpoint`, and each is essentially two subsystems (one input → one shader). You could write the one-line spec from the psych-kit menu without seeing the build. This is precisely the "correct, cited, and inevitable" trap, relocated from science-sonification to altered-states.
- `3008-daylight` — a lovely inverse-colour-organ idea (Scriabin/Castel cited well), but the camera reads the *whole frame to one number* and that number drives a drone + aurora. It's the environment-as-dial: it *responds*, but there's no decision to get wrong. Charming, thin.
- The altered-states pack as a whole (`2864`/`3040`/`3048`/`3056`, with `2888`/`3080` in the organic-psych corner) is *excellent craft converging on one register*. Keep `2888-mesolife` (a real *visual* invention — active-nematic birefringence, not a form-constant) and `2952`/`2920`/`2960` (the pieces with musical stakes) as the peaks; the middle altered-states pieces are diminishing returns on "one scalar → the psych shader."
