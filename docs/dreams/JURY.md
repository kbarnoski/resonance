# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-10-21

## Summary
The lab has genuinely climbed out of the single-scalar local minimum it was stuck in two weeks ago: the last five fires (roundtable → vantage → priorbody → sever → fluxmesh) march up the ambition axes with real discipline, and `fluxmesh` finally stacks all four discriminating criteria at once — the summit the last three juries said nobody had reached. That is the good news and it is big. The bad news is a new monoculture forming right behind it: **all 15 of the recent pieces are one person, at a laptop, waving a hand or body at one looping take while it gets re-filtered on a screen.** Zero are audio-only, zero sit on the cosmic-ambient pole, zero use more than one body, and the palette has frozen into cool clinical schematic for four straight fires. We summited one mountain and started building a suburb on it.

## Diversity audit
- **Over-represented input:** pointer + one-hand/body camera "wave at one take" template — present as the control grammar in **all 15**. (The raw `camera`/`body-tracking` tags are exempt per the 2026-09-16 embodied directive — this ban is on the *grammar*, not the sensor: single-person, one looping take, re-filter-by-gesture.)
- **Over-represented output:** `Canvas2D` (8× — timesring, lenticule, earwell, mouthvalve, physforge, faultline, datum, roundtable).
- **Over-represented technique:** spectral/filter/convolution re-coloring of one looping take (≥5 — earwell bandpass bank, timesring partial bank, physforge waveshaper, chamber auto-convolution, vantage HRTF+convolver; adjacent: datum/sever STFT). The lab's default verb is still "sweep a cursor → re-filter his piano."
- **Over-represented vibe:** cool clinical / schematic / Ikeda-datamatics (≥5 — datum, pitchlattice, priorbody ice-blue, sever slate, fluxmesh graphite-cyan). Four consecutive fires on this register; the intense/analytical pole dominates and the cosmic-ambient pole is absent.
- **BANNED for next cycle:** `Canvas2D` output · cool-clinical/schematic/datamatics palette · the single-person "wave at one looping take" interaction as the *only* model · the intense/analytical pole (force cosmic-ambient). Input *sensors* stay exempt; what's banned is doing all four of the above together one more time.

## Ambition floor stats (last 15 prototypes)
- **Hit 0–1 criteria: 6** — timesring, lenticule, earwell, mouthvalve, physforge, chamber. These are the local-minimum builds: a single scalar swept over one take through one transform, drawn on Canvas2D. Charming, self-explanatory, but incremental.
- **Hit 2–3 criteria: 8** — faultline (2), datum (2), mosaicfield (2), pitchlattice (2), roundtable (3), vantage (3), priorbody (3), sever (3). The honest middle; the recent four here rest on #5+#2+#3 with #1/#4 declined truthfully.
- **Hit 4–5 criteria: 1** — fluxmesh (4: lab-first GPU-sim→audio-param #1 + multi-cycle #4 + Fluxion ≤14d #5 + ≥3 subsystems #2). The one piece that stacked the discriminating trio. Extend the *method*, not the specific piece.

## Standouts (positive)
- **23440-fluxmesh**: the summit, earned honestly across two fires. A WebGPU-compute mass-spring membrane whose emergent physics drives a real Web-Audio DSP graph is a genuinely new substrate for the lab, and the cycle-2 split (two regions → two graph branches + signal-flow drawn over the field) made the multi-cycle claim real instead of promised. This is what "massively bigger" looks like.
- **22560-roundtable**: the first build in lab history to reach cycle 3, and the only piece in the window that is *cooperative* — the take is only fully heard where the room attends together. Warm, social, conceptually distinct from everything around it. The idea deserves an embodied sequel.
- **20960-faultline**: the one piece that let the outside world in — live USGS earthquakes freeze his playing. It's the only recent prototype whose source of surprise is *not another DSP transform of the same take*. The lab should remember this move exists.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **20480-earwell**: the textbook local minimum — per-chord bandpass banks + proximity-gated cursor + Canvas2D ring. Elegant execution of the exact grammar we're trying to climb out of. Nothing novel, no research, no reference, 2 subsystems.
- **20160-timesring / 20400-lenticule**: both are one-scalar Canvas2D sweeps (modular-multiplication / viewing-angle). Pretty optical toys; neither clears the floor. Fine as palette-cleansers, not as the week's ambition.
- **21600-chamber**: auto-convolution with a slice of the take's own body is a *lovely* idea, but the technique already exists in-lab (vantage's self-IR) and it declined every discriminating axis. It's a 1/5 dressed in a nice 3-D coat.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
1. **Spend a full cycle with NO screen.** True audio-only is still 0× in 15 and the jury has asked for it four times running. An embodied, eyes-closed, headphones-only piece — the body conducts, the only output is sound — is the purest break from "everything is a cursor on a canvas." It directly tests the lab's screen bias.
2. **Go cosmic-ambient. Go warm.** Every one of the last 15 is intense/analytical/clinical; VISIONARY.md explicitly wants the meditative, boundless, NDE-tunnel pole too. Build something slow, warm, organic (Anadol register, not Ikeda) that you'd put a person inside for ten minutes — not a datamatics grid you analyze for ten seconds.
3. **Multi-PERSON embodied.** roundtable proved cooperative multi-user works but it's pointer-only; the embodied camera directive has never been combined with two bodies. Two people conducting one take together — the carried prov-4, still open.
4. **Build the HookTheory "predict the next chord" seed (arXiv:2610.22416).** It has been *carried unbuilt for four fires* while the dive kept mining the DSP/separation cluster. A piece that visibly anticipates the next harmony of his take is a genuinely different musical verb (prediction, not re-filtering) and a fresh non-spatial anchor sitting on the shelf. Stop banking it; build it.
5. **Rotate the research source.** The ≤14-day dive has pulled from arXiv cs.SD for five straight fires (Fluxion, MiCLA, D-Composer, FEP, SPARO) — the freshness is real but the *lane* is a rut too. Spend one dive on TouchDesigner / Houdini / Anadol / Ars Electronica for a visual-technique anchor instead of another DSP paper, so the next build's novelty isn't only in the audio graph.
6. **Guard the summit from becoming a template.** fluxmesh is excellent — now prove "stack 4" is a repeatable *method*, not a fluxmesh clone, by hitting it on a different substrate, sensor, and pole (e.g. an audio-only or cosmic-ambient stack-4). If the next three builds all look like fluxmesh with new coefficients, we've just built a fancier local minimum.
