## Standouts (positive)
- **14656-answerback** — the sharpest concept of the window and the direct cash of the last verdict's #2. A call-and-**response** retrieval duet: you play a phrase, the machine *answers* with the closest-matching phrase from across your whole catalog, sliced from the real audio. First MIDI, first cross-catalog query-by-contour retrieval, and — critically — a genuinely *new verb* (converse/compose) that isn't conduct/mix/transform. This is the one piece that both broke the meta-rut and hit the standing provocation.
- **14704-choirglass** — the biggest visual-register break available: a luminous full-chromatic inline-SVG rose window instead of the lab's shader-field norm, driven by voice-as-harmony-controller (a fresh 0×-in-window input). It cashes the last verdict's literal "full chromatic spectrum" ask and is the most legible unattended-morning demo of the fifteen. Rule-10 clean (mic dead-ended, 100% audible sound is his catalog).
- **14336-tidalstrata** — the lab's first honest long-form flagship: a ~10-minute two-timescale piece with real state and memory that is *audibly different at minute five than minute one*. The "long-form generative with state/evolution" menu item has been thin for months; this is the one that actually filled it. (It is still a whole-catalog piece, but the long-form-memory axis excuses it — this is the rut done at its most ambitious.)
- **14576-reefmind** — the only piece that broke the social contract everything else in the window shares: an autonomous Gray-Scott membrane that *arranges itself*, no controller, no conductor. A fresh visual language (morphogenetic spots↔stripes↔mazes) and a return to the starved cosmic-ambient PRIMARY DIRECTION. Its concept is the antidote to the meta-rut, even if it's one of the six.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule still holds)
- **14256-mididuet** — the weakest of the concatenative cluster and the clearest local minimum. It's the middle child of three "play your catalog out of slices of itself" pieces: notemirror does it *purer* (discrete real note-slices), answerback does it *bigger* (cross-catalog retrieval + a new verb), and mididuet sits between them — grains of your own timbre over a single bed — adding little the other two don't. A duet-with-yourself that's really a third take on the same mechanism.
- **14224-skyflock** — a competent whole-catalog mixer, but its controller (solar position) barely moves over a session, so as an *instrument* it's nearly static — a fader bank driven by a dial that turns once a day. It's the "whole-catalog-conducted-by-X" template at its least playable, and the second real-world-data-arrangement piece in the window's span.
- **14400-orbitcompass** — sits squarely inside both ruts at once: a heading-driven 16-track fader bank (meta-template) painted warm-dawn-amber (palette ban). The HRTF spatial ring is nicely built, but concept-wise it is exactly the average of the window rather than an escape from it.
