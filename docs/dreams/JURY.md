# Concept Jury Verdict — 2026-08-01

## Summary
The ambition floor is holding — 14 of the last 15 cleared it, one true 4/5
(`4520-seismarium`) and one genuine first-in-lab technique (`4616-pressing`, Web
MIDI). But the monoculture the last jury named didn't get fixed, it got
*swapped*: yesterday the lab was a neuroscience-of-perception gallery; today it's
a **restraint-and-consequence chapel**. Four of the last window's pieces
(`4440-vow`, `4472-reef`, `4568-marble`, `4616-pressing`, plus `4376-drag`'s
lock-or-lose) are the same emotional move — *"you can't take it back"* — re-skinned
across five renderers, and seven of fifteen ship as inline SVG/DOM. The lab found
one resonant frame and one cheap renderer and is now farming both. The floor isn't
the problem. The lab keeps solving *taste* and forgetting *range*.

## Diversity audit
- Over-represented input: **keyboard / symbolic-note-event** (7× — `4216`, `4296`,
  `4376`, `4472`, `4616`, `4680`, `4728`; 5 of them keyboard-primary)
- Over-represented output: **inline SVG / DOM** (7× — `4232`, `4376`, `4472`,
  `4568`, `4616`, `4680`, `4776`)
- Over-represented technique: **symbolic-note-event Web-Audio synth, no-FFT/no-mic
  "playable instrument"** (7× — the 4296/4376/4472/4616/4680/4728 family + 4440)
- Over-represented vibe: **conceptual-critical "irreversibility / restraint / cost"**
  (5–6× — `4376`, `4440`, `4472`, `4568`, `4616`, adjacent `4680`)
- **BANNED for next cycle:** keyboard/symbolic-note input · inline-SVG/DOM output ·
  no-FFT symbolic-instrument technique · restraint/no-undo/scarcity conceptual vibe.
  A keyboard-played, SVG-rendered, "you-can't-take-it-back" instrument is this
  window's local minimum. Do not mint another.

## Standouts (positive)
- `4520-seismarium`: the window's peak. The living Earth as a self-playing
  instrument — real USGS feed → WebGPU 2D wave-equation basin with genuine
  accumulating memory → modal bells snapped to a slowly-rotating pentatonic
  grammar so an endless stream stays *music*, not a siren. This is what
  "real-world data sonification" was supposed to look like.
- `4616-pressing`: the first Web MIDI / MPE integration in lab history — an input
  class flagged as starved for cycles and finally cashed — wrapped in the cleanest
  version of the one-take/etched-forever idea (the cut *is* the signal, Lucier as
  the ref). The novelty is real, not costume.
- `4776-contour`: the freshest technique family of the window — a droplet
  hydraulic-erosion sim rendered as a live marching-squares topographic map whose
  strongest channel sings itself into consonance as the drainage tree matures. A
  genuinely new metaphor and a ready-made long-form arc.
- `4264-lucent`: finally honors the standing "feed it Karel's REAL Path piano"
  directive — a WebGL2 flow-field with actual long-form memory (minute 5 ≠ minute
  1), his recording painting the light. (Yesterday's jury praised it too; it earns
  the repeat.)
- `4728-rubato`: a score-free, click-free, ML-free accompanist that *breathes with
  your rubato* via a Large & Jones attending oscillator. The rare conceptual build
  here whose stakes are musical, not moral.

## Pruning candidates (concept-level, NOT for deletion — immutability rule holds)
- `4232-pendulum`: the one sub-floor build. "Consonance made visible" via a
  harmonograph is well-trodden, the `-pendulum` slug keeps recurring across cycles,
  and there's no research chain or citable reference carrying it. This is the
  pentatonic-plus-visualizer of the window.
- `4568-marble`: not weak on its own — but by the time `4440-vow` and
  `4616-pressing` exist, "carve/commit and you can't undo it" is the *third*
  restatement of the same idea in one window. The subtract-from-saturation framing
  is the only genuinely fresh part; the restraint concept around it is now inventory.
- The **restraint/irreversibility cluster as a whole** is the concept-level rut:
  `vow`, `reef`, `marble`, `pressing` are four costumes on one moral. Individually
  defensible; collectively the exact "local minimum in a prestige costume" the last
  two juries warned about, now wearing sackcloth instead of a lab coat.

## Provocations for tomorrow's dream cycle
- **The restraint frame is spent — ban "you can't take it back" for a week.** It
  was moving at `4440-vow`. By `4616-pressing` it's a genre. Force a build with no
  scarcity, no permanence, no penance meter.
- **Get off SVG and off the keyboard.** SVG/DOM is 7 of 15 and keyboard/symbolic-note
  is 7 of 15 — the cheapest renderer and the cheapest input, both maxed. WebGPU
  compute is only 2 of 15 (`4360`, `4520`) and depth-camera / body-tracking is
  **still 0×**. Spend a cycle on a sensor the lab keeps avoiding.
- **The psychedelic *primary* direction is fading again** — only 3 of the last 15
  touch altered-states (`4200`, `4216`, `4264`), and `4216` reused the log-polar
  engine rather than a fresh technique. AGENT.md calls this the primary direction;
  it's drifting back to adult-instrument/conceptual. Return to the INTENSE or
  cosmic-ambient pole with a technique that isn't log-polar.
- **The spatial/installation lane is a graveyard of banked ideas.** `3912-atrium`,
  `3928-vespers`, `3904-lodestar` were all banked "ship-next" for the room/embodied
  gap and none has resurrected. Either build the room or stop banking it — a
  perpetually-queued idea is a lie the ledger tells itself.
- **Extend the two peaks instead of minting a sixteenth.** `4520-seismarium` wants
  a multi-source planetary instrument (fold in the TRAPPIST/space-weather streams
  already researched); `4776-contour` wants a second face or a two-viewer erosion.
  AGENT.md says read the jury and go DEEP on what it names — this is the name.
- **The AI-pipeline chain (music→image→video) has been "queued next" for ~10
  cycles and every prior jury.** Stop listing it. Put a yes/no to Karel: fund
  `FAL_KEY` and build it, or strike it from the queue permanently.

wall of SVG — but `4520-seismarium` (the live Earth, singing) and `4616-pressing` (our
first-ever MIDI) prove it can still surprise; push it off keyboard-and-SVG and into the
camera/room/psychedelic lanes it keeps banking and never building.
