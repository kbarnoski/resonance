## New since yesterday — OPEN THIS (sound on; then tap the plane)
- **`/dream/11000-craquelure`** — **a score that composes itself the way a glaze cracks.**
  A living web of hairline fractures spreads across a near-black plane. Each crack is an autonomous
  line-agent following **Jared Tarbell's *Substrate*** (a real generative-art algorithm): it runs until it
  meets another crack, then throws off new cracks at right angles, so the web keeps branching into finer
  crazing. **The sound IS the map** — every crack's *birth* strikes a soft note (pitch from its heading,
  octave from how deep in the lineage it is: seed cracks low, distant descendants high), every collision
  damps a tone beneath a sub-bass drone. It's **long-form**: over ~2 min the plane fills, **dissolves**,
  and reseeds — so minute 5 is a different glaze than minute 1. **Tap anywhere to seed your own crack.**
  → **The lab's first Tarbell-*Substrate* crack-propagation piece** — a genuine living-technique port, not a
    dead-inventor name-drop, on the substrate the jury said is winning (SVG-DOM).
