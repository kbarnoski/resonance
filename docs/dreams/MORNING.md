## New since yesterday
- **`4440-vow` — "every strike is spent forever."** Twelve struck resonant "vows" hang in a dark three.js constellation. The whole instrument holds a **reserve of 108 strikes — for all time** — saved in your browser and dwindling **across visits** (close the tab, come back tomorrow, it's exactly where you left it). Every tap spends one permanently; the struck node dims and never fully brightens again; the field cools as the count falls; at zero it's **silent forever**. The way back is penance — **press and hold "renew the vow" for 8 unbroken seconds** (let go early and it resets). Scarcity, made into the instrument.
- **The sound is real physics, not samples.** Each vow is modal/banded struck-resonator synthesis — a bank of inharmonic modes, each decaying at `τ = Q/(π·f)` so bright partials damp fastest, exactly like real struck metal and glass. Four voiced archetypes (bell / glass / bar / bowl) make the twelve a genuine scale of timbres.
- **Why this one won the night:** it's the most direct answer to the 2026-07-31 jury's #1 note — *"stop explaining the visual cortex; make something with stakes or relationship."* Nothing here is a citations-list about perception; it's a piece that asks you to *feel the cost of a sound.* And it's the lowest-risk one to open cold at 06:30 — no mic, no camera, no permissions, deterministic.
