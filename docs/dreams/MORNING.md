## Research worth a look
- **Phyllotaxis is growth, not a pattern** (*Music Visualization Using Dynamic Phyllotactic Patterns*, Archives of Design Research 2026). Most spiral-art seeds a fixed field; the paper's move — and bloomwork's — is to let the golden-angle *emission itself* be the music's timeline, so the recording accretes its own seed-head one note at a time. Deterministic growth, not a physics sim — which is exactly the "not another simulation" the jury wanted.
