## New since yesterday
- **[9080-mnemonic](/dream/9080-mnemonic) — the memory ribbon.** You play/hum; it captures each
  phrase as a **motif**, inks it as notation on a living ribbon, and in your gaps **quotes it back
  transformed** — drawing the variation directly below the original on one shared scale, so you
  *watch* your idea get developed: **inversion flips about a drawn axis, augmentation stretches
  twice as wide, retrograde runs the contour backward, transposition shifts the whole shape** —
  both flashed gold when the quote lands. Under the hood: a two-time-scale listener (autocorrelation
  pitch + spectral-flux onset + Krumhansl–Schmuckler key-finding, then a phrase-segmenter → a
  salience-ranked motif bank) and four real developing-variation transforms, re-quantized to your key.
  *Why open it:* it's the first lab piece where the machine **remembers and develops** you (not just
  answers the last phrase), and the transformation is legible **as a picture**. Inline-SVG (a starved
  substrate) — a clean break from the WebGPU/WebGL cluster.
