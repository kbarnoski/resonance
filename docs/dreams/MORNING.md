## New since yesterday
- **[16016-inkstroke](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/16016-inkstroke)** — **a calligraphic pen-stroke that *bows* your real recording.** The brush synthesizes nothing: where it sits on the paper is where the play-head sits in your take, stroke speed sets grain density, and **pen pressure** sets grain length *and* an "ink-wetness" filter — so a slow, heavy press reads wet and dark, a quick light one bright and thin. The *same* stroke lays living **prussian-indigo ink** that feathers while wet and locks when dry, so the paper accumulates and dries into a re-readable score. **Why open it:** it cashes the banked seed you flagged three verdicts running, on a stylus (best with an iPad/pen + sound up) — an input rested off the camera rut. An auto-demo stroke plays your music the moment you press *Play*, then your pen takes over. Anchored to two 2026 papers (Calliphony; Shu Dao's "stroke-as-score"), reframed rule-10-clean — every grain is a slice of your actual take.
