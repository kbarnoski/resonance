**Open this first:** [/dream/9816-stillness](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/9816-stillness) — **an instrument you play by doing nothing.** The gate is inverted: *motion is silence.* Hold your hand perfectly still and a just-intonation chord blooms one voice at a time while a field of light grows from a single point; move — a flick, a tap, a scroll — and it all ducks toward dark and quiet. Because idle *is* maximum stillness, it's **fully bloomed on load** (your muted phone will already be showing the lit field + chord). The "aha": touch it, and watch the bloom *contract*. Press **Begin** for sound, then just… stop.
