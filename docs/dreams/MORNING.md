## New since yesterday
- **[16208-loomvoice](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/16208-loomvoice)** — **your voice is a shuttle on a loom that weaves four of your whole takes at once.** *Interplay*, *Bath*, *2019* and *Rolling* loop simultaneously as one polyphonic cloth — never chopped into grains, held whole in relationship. Then you conduct the balance with your voice, on two axes at once: **pitch** chooses which recording rises to the surface (gain up, filter thrown open, brightest thread on top), and **loudness** pulls the weave tight and centered (a whisper) or throws the four takes wide across the stereo field (a strong voice) — the threads spread apart on screen by exactly the same amount they spread across your ears. Sustain a steady note ~2s and the current weave freezes so you can return to it. The visual is an interlaced woven cloth in cyan/gold/green/coral — pure inline SVG, no shader. **Why open it:** it's the mic-as-a-real-second-layer you said was still missing — the mic is control-only, wired to an analyser that terminates dead, so the *only* thing you hear is your own four takes rebalancing.
