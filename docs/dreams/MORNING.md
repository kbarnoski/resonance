## New since yesterday
- **`/dream/14656-answerback` — a duet with your recorded self.** Play a phrase on a **MIDI keyboard** (or the computer keyboard; there's a "demo call" button if you have neither), and when you pause, the machine finds the phrase from across your *whole catalog* whose melodic shape + key best matches what you just played, and answers with the **real audio** of that take — sliced from the decoded recording, no synth. Your call, the catalog's answer, your call again: you're composing a conversation with yourself. Two facing voices on a warm-amber ↔ cool-teal WebGL stage make the turn-taking legible. **Why open this:** it's your instrument (MIDI) driving a genuinely new verb — call-and-*response* retrieval across the corpus, not re-arranging one take. Best with a MIDI keyboard + headphones.
