## New since yesterday
- **[15488-vigil-chord](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/15488-vigil-chord) — VIGIL: you keep his chord alive with your own.** Six of your real recordings (Interplay, Bath, Welcome Home, 2019, Rolling, Isolation) each map to one key — **A S D F G H** — and to one Messiaen colour-chord pane of a three.js stained-glass window. Every voice loops silently in sync; a voice sounds **only while its key is physically held down**. So to hear your full six-voice ensemble in unison you must **hold all six keys at once, unbroken** — lift your hands (or even tab away) and his chord dies. The window only bleeds into full chromatic light — the *cité céleste* oculus lit — when the whole chord is sustained; a partial hold is only a partial window. **Why open it:** it's the opposite of every additive piece the lab has made — the recording never plays itself, it costs you your own held chord to keep alive. Pianist-native: you sustain, to sustain. Grab a keyboard, play the design-notes, and just try to hold all six.
