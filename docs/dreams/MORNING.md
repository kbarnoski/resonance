# Morning digest — last updated 2026-07-25 (cycle 899, DEEP)

## New since yesterday
- **[2656-loom](/dream/2656-loom)** — *a piece of music that remembers, and returns.* A long-form generative organism you **tend, not play**: it keeps an explicit, growing **library of little motifs** and weaves the future out of the past — recalling old motifs transformed (inverted, augmented, fragmented, pushed chromatically off the grid). Over ~8 minutes it drifts so far from where it began that minute 8 no longer *sounds like* second 0 — and then, near the end, it **recapitulates**: the very first phrase returns, recognizable but transformed, so the arc closes. Leave it running (visuals animate on load; press **Begin** for sound), or **plant a seed contour**, nudge dissonance ±, or hit **"Jump ahead 4 min"** to fast-forward the development.
  - Why open this: it fills the lab's **rarest lane** — long-form / evolving, which the jury said was *"still held by one piece."* And it goes one better: it's the only piece here that **recapitulates**, the real proof of long-form memory. This is also the strongest cash yet of "let a piece grow and *return*," not loop.
  - The tell is on screen, muted: a **memory loom** where every motif is a little contour glyph, threaded to its descendants — you literally watch a motif be born, deformed, and finally recalled. A tension band arcs up to a dissonant climax around minute 5 and resolves at the close (verified numerically: measured dissonance 0.00→0.81→0.0; the recap recalls the opening seed).
  - **The one thing I need your ear on:** does the **recapitulation land emotionally as a return** — do you recognize the opening when it comes back? And does the dissonant climax read as *tension* rather than noise? Those are the perceptual claims I can't test headless.

## Explored but not shipped (2 more — see IDEAS §899)
- **2642-grove** ⭐⭐ — *music that grows like a plant.* The same "long-form organism you tend" idea, but built as a genuine **musical L-system** and drawn as a **branching tree that visibly grows** as the music derives (recurring motifs share a branch-hue, so you see the memory). Gorgeous, and the most instantly-legible of the three — held only because the loom realizes the *recapitulation* more fully and I could verify its arc headless. The natural next move is to **fuse them**: the loom's rigorous memory-and-return engine, drawn as grove's living tree.

The tightest tie to this week's research (a hand-built version of a steerable concept space). Held because the WebGL2 trail is the most abstract of the three when muted.
