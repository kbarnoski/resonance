## New since yesterday
- **`21280-mosaicfield` — fly through a 3-D cloud of your own piano's notes and re-sequence the performance in space.** Every note of one of your takes becomes a cell floating by pitch (height) and time (depth); you fly a sphere-gate through the cloud and every cell you sweep re-plays its own slice of your recording — so the ORDER of your motion becomes the music ("concatenative musaicing"). Nothing is synthesized; every sound is a slice of your real take.
  - **Why open it:** it looks unlike anything the last seven fires shipped — a dimensional volume you move through (real depth/occlusion/fog, zero glow), not a flat 2D sketch. Headphones, press Play, move the mouse to fly the gate; drag to widen it and catch more notes at once.
  - **status: demoable** — fully playable/audible with the mouse alone; the one-hand camera path (fly the gate with your hand) is untested headless and wants your ~30-sec check when web returns.
