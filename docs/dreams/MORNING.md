## New since yesterday
- **[15424-negative](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/15424-negative) — THE NEGATIVE SCORE: hear the *silences* of your recording, your notes ducked to a ghost.** In 1,184 pieces the lab has always been additive — it reads, transforms, or recomposes the notes you PLAYED. This is the first to make the **negative space the subject**: it removes your notes and foregrounds everything between them — the rests, the pedal-lift air, the breath, the reverb tails, the room minus the playing. One real take (default *Bath*) plays through an **inverted-gain envelope**: your notes duck to a floor, your silences open to full — 0 synth, it only gates your own recording. **Why open it:** the Canvas2D surface is an *inverted piano roll* — your actual notes are **dark holes punched out of a luminous full-chromatic field** (color = your harmony). Where you play, the ribbon goes dark; where you're silent, it glows. You see and hear the photographic negative of your own playing. Drag **Reveal** up to fade your notes back in and A-B exactly what's been taken away. This is the jury's "emptiness room" — the still-open half of provocation #2 (`15344-toll` took the "cost" half). *Note: at Reveal 0 it's intentionally near-silent — nudge Reveal/Air up to confirm sound is flowing.*

## Research findings worth a look
- **The music of silence** — *The Music of Silence* (Di Liberto et al., J Neurosci 2021), the mechanism that seeded today's build: when the music stops, your brain keeps responding — and the response to the **silence has INVERTED polarity** relative to a heard note, yet its size precisely tracks the melodic **expectation of the missing note**. The negative space isn't empty; it's where your cortex composes the ghost of what should be there. The inverted-gain envelope literally plays that signal. (Corroborated by 2026 arrangement writing framing negative space as the primary compositional material.)
