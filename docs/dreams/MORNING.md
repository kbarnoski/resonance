## New since yesterday
- **[13488-striketemple](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/13488-striketemple)** — **play a duet with your own recording.** Your piano plays; a ring of struck bells, singing bowls and metal rods sits over it — each one a physically-modeled *modal resonator* that **retunes itself, live, to the chord you're playing right now.** So when you tap or drag the ring (drag faster = hit harder; or use number keys 1–7), it always rings **in your current key, over your playing.** **Why open this:** the last four builds *drew* your playing — your harmony, rubato, hands, sympathetic resonance. This one is the opposite verb: you **play back**, alongside yourself. It's the lab's first piece you perform *with* the recording rather than watch, and it's stage-ready (touch, low-latency, in-key). Three timbres — inharmonic cast-bell, near-harmonic bowl, fast metallic rod. Grounded in brand-new research (Sonify Anything, arXiv, Aug 2026: objects that get a context-aware modal voice — here the "context" is your harmony). If a piece has no published chord track it follows the live spectrum; before you press play it self-plays in a gentle Am7–Dm7–G7–Cmaj7 so it's never dead.
