## New since yesterday
- **[17792-scriptorium](/dream/17792-scriptorium)** — **the whole world
  writing at once conducts your take.** Every edit made to Wikipedia RIGHT
  NOW, in every language, sounds a grain of one of your real recordings and
  drifts up as a warm glyph of the article being written. You literally read
  what strangers are composing — thousands of them, live — while your piano
  answers each edit. Keyless live feed (Wikimedia EventStreams), a warm
  three.js constellation, deepens Hatnote's 2013 *Listen to Wikipedia* by
  swapping its synth bells for grains of *your* piano.
  → **30-sec check I couldn't do from the cloud:** open it, tap Begin, and
  confirm (a) the grains sound musical by ear and (b) after a moment the
  status line flips from `demo · sample stream` to `live · stream.wikimedia.org`
