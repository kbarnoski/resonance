## New since yesterday
- **`2808-palimpsest` — your movement draws a musical score you can both see and hear.**
  The webcam reads your body one column at a time, like a moving spectrogram. Your motion
  **accretes into a persistent violet score** that slowly decays — a true palimpsest, so a
  gesture you drew a minute ago is still faintly there. A **playhead sweeps** the score and
  reads each column back through a 32-partial additive bank (height = pitch, continuous, never
  snapped to a scale). As the playhead re-crosses old strata, **they sound again — a canon of
  your own drawing** (Steve Reich's phase idea, made from your body; Ikeda / Xenakis UPIC for
  the look). **Why open it:** it's the first piece where the *memory is a growing thing you can
  see* — a real beginning→middle→end that's a different piece at minute 5 than second 0, and the
  cleanest answer yet to "get off the keyboard, use the camera." Draws + sounds on load with no
  camera (seeded virtual performer); grant the camera and move to play it. No strobe.
