## New since yesterday
- **`3392-longnow` → [/dream/3392-longnow](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/3392-longnow)** — **a piece of music on a 1000-year clock.** Its entire state is a deterministic function of real wall-clock time since a fixed epoch, so it never repeats in a lifetime — *and everyone who opens the page at the same instant drops into the same "now" of it.* You don't play it, can't win/lose/break it; you **visit** it, in quiet company with unseen others. Six ambient layers cycle at coprime-prime periods (3 min → 1 year), so their combination doesn't recur for ~10³⁰ seconds — Jem Finer's actual *Longplayer* mechanism, plus a nod to Cage's *ASLSP* and Eno. *Why open it:* it's the jury's no-stakes ask AND its "shared listening room" idea — but with **zero blind-push risk** (synchrony through a shared clock, not WebRTC). Press **Listen to the now**; the chord audibly migrates over 30–90 s. Use the **Time telescope** (±1 hr / day / yr / 100 yr) to hear a century from now, then **Return to the now**. First deep-time engine in the lab (0×), first no-stakes piece in ~15 cycles, audio-primary on an SVG orrery (not the banned Canvas2D).
