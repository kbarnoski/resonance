## New since yesterday
- **`3528-wrest`** — /dream/3528-wrest — **a third way to relate to an instrument: not *play* it, not *watch* it, but continuously *wrest* it back from itself.** One voice is always singing and drifting on its own (its own momentum, softly pulled through a rotating chord field — never snapped to a scale). Hum, and control slides to *you* in ~0.1 s; go quiet and it slides back to the machine over ~1.5 s — so you can **rescue** it from wherever it wandered, then let it roam. It keeps a slow **memory** of where you pulled it, so minute five is the record of how you negotiated. **Real agency, real consequence — but no score, no win/lose, no fail-buzzer.** Why open it: it's the missing register — the jury and I both flagged that "no-stakes ambient" had become the rut; this is stakes that aren't a fail-buzzer. WebGL2 flow-field; self-demos with a synthetic performer if you don't grant the mic.
