## New since yesterday
- **[14240-inkscore](https://getresonance.vercel.app/dream/14240-inkscore)** — **write a new piece from your whole catalog, in ink on paper.** Play the computer keyboard like a composer, not a pianist: number keys `1–8` pick which recording the next phrase comes from, **SPACE** drops the "next best in-key" phrase-slice of your real audio onto a warm **paper-white graphic score** (Cardew *Treatise* / Xenakis UPIC lineage — expressive ink marks: height = register, length = duration, weight = energy). A sienna playhead sweeps and **re-triggers each mark as it passes**, so the score you're drawing *is* the instrument. A **harmony engine** (Krumhansl key-profiles) selects phrases that fit a running key and gently snaps them in tune — and the **consonance slider** crossfades live from raw collage (0) to fully in-key (1). **Why open this:** first sound is one keypress (or click "Seed a phrase"); then push the consonance slider and re-key the piece to hear a pile of your phrases resolve into music. Every mark is a real slice of your catalog — zero synthesis.
